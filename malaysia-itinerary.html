Day 2 · Thu, May 1 · Hong Kong
Arrive Hong Kong
Land at HKG 06:15 · Check in at Harbour Grand Hong Kong · Rest, explore
🏨 Harbour Grand Hong Kong · Check-in 15:00
🌅 Morning (9:00 – 12:30)
9:00
Lin Heung Tea House (蓮香樓)
Classic dim sum parlor with cart service in Sheung Wan. Old-school HK experience — get there early, locals queue.
Food
10:00
Hong Kong Museum of History (香港歷史博物館)
MTR to Tsim Sha Tsui. "Hong Kong Story" permanent exhibition — 400M years of natural history through the handover. Allow ~2 hours.
Museum
12:00
Tsim Sha Tsui Promenade
Victoria Harbour waterfront, Clock Tower (1915 KCR railway relic), Avenue of Stars.
Walk
☀️ Afternoon (12:30 – 17:30)
12:30
Mak's Noodle / Tsim Chai Kee (中環)
Star Ferry across to Central (HK$3 — an experience itself). Wonton noodles in a no-frills shop.
Food
13:30
PMQ (元創方)
Former Police Married Quarters on Hollywood Road, now creative arts hub. Free — galleries, design studios, rotating exhibitions.
Culture
14:00
Hollywood Road & Sheung Wan
Man Mo Temple (1847, incense coils, Taoist/Buddhist), antique shops, dried seafood street on Des Voeux Road West.
Culture
15:30
Dr Sun Yat-sen Museum (孫中山紀念館)
Kom Tong Hall (Edwardian, declared monument). Sun's ties to HK and the revolutionary movement.
Museum
16:30
Tai Kwun Centre for Heritage and Arts (大館)
Former Central Police Station (1841–2006). Three declared monuments, contemporary art galleries (free), judicial history exhibits.
Museum
🌆 Evening (17:30 – 21:00)
17:30
Wan Chai Heritage Trail
Tram from Central. Blue House Cluster (Grade 1 historic tenement, living museum), Pak Tai Temple (1863), old market streets.
Culture
18:30
Ser Wong Fun (蛇王芬)
Operating since 1895 — snake soup, clay pot rice, old HK comfort food. Alt: Under Bridge Spicy Crab for typhoon shelter crab.
Food
20:00
Symphony of Lights / Night Tram Ride
8pm light show from TST waterfront, or ding-ding tram end-to-end through neon-lit Wan Chai → North Point. Nightcap at a dai pai dong.
Walk
Tips
  • Octopus card — covers MTR, trams, Star Ferry, 7-Eleven
  • Museum hours — most close Tuesdays; check before going
  • Weather — early May is warm & humid, carry water and umbrella
  • Free admission — Tai Kwun, PMQ, and some Museum of History exhibits

Day 4 · Sat, May 3 · Hong Kong / Macau
Hong Kong / Macau
Day trip to Macau — UNESCO Historic Centre, museums, Portuguese egg tarts · Check out by 12:00
🏨 Harbour Grand Hong Kong · Check-out 12:00
🌅 Morning (7:00 – 12:00)
7:00
Ferry to Macau
TurboJET from Sheung Wan (Shun Tak Centre) to Macau Outer Harbour. ~55 min. Book in advance on weekends.
Transport
8:15
Breakfast: Margaret's Cafe e Nata (瑪嘉烈蛋撻)
The original Portuguese egg tart in Macau. Flaky pastry, caramelised custard. Tiny shop near Senado Square — cash only.
Food
9:00
Ruins of St. Paul's (大三巴牌坊)
Iconic 1602 church facade — UNESCO World Heritage. Adjacent Museum of Sacred Art in the crypt has religious artifacts and bones of Japanese/Vietnamese martyrs.
Museum
9:45
Monte Fort & Macau Museum (大炮台 · 澳門博物館)
17th-century fortress with panoramic views. The museum inside covers 400 years of Macau's East-meets-West history — Portuguese colonisation, Chinese traditions, multicultural daily life.
Museum
11:00
Senado Square & Holy House of Mercy (議事亭前地)
Wave-patterned Portuguese pavement, pastel neoclassical buildings. Holy House of Mercy (1569) is one of Asia's oldest charitable institutions — small museum upstairs.
Culture
☀️ Afternoon (12:00 – 17:00)
12:00
Lunch: Restaurante Litoral Taipa (海灣餐廳 · 氹仔)
Portuguese & cha chaan teng fare in Taipa Old Village (氹仔舊城區). $101-200 MOP range. Note: check opening hours — may be closed at certain times (休息中).
Food
13:30
Mandarin's House (鄭家大屋)
1869 compound of writer Zheng Guanying — largest traditional Chinese residential complex in Macau. Free entry. Beautiful courtyard blending Chinese and Western architecture.
Culture
14:15
A-Ma Temple (媽閣廟)
Oldest temple in Macau (1488), dedicated to the sea goddess Mazu. Macau's name derives from "A-Ma-Gao." Incense-filled halls built into the hillside.
Culture
14:45
Maritime Museum (海事博物館)
Right next to A-Ma Temple. Covers Macau's seafaring history, Chinese junk boats, Portuguese Age of Discovery. Small but well curated.
Museum
15:30
Taipa Village (氹仔舊城區)
Bus or taxi to Taipa. Walk Rua do Cunha (Food Street) — pork chop buns, almond cookies, serradura. Visit Taipa Houses Museum (colonial Portuguese villas turned museum).
Culture
16:15
Snack: Lord Stow's Bakery (Taipa) (安德魯餅店)
The other legendary egg tart — Lord Stow's originated in Coloane. Creamy English-style custard in a flaky shell. Compare with Margaret's from this morning.
Food
🌆 Evening (17:00 – 20:30)
17:00
Cotai Strip Walk
Stroll past the Venetian, Parisian, and City of Dreams. Over-the-top architecture worth seeing even if you don't gamble. Free entry to lobbies.
Walk
18:00
Dinner: Chan Seng Kei (陳勝記)
In Coloane Village — known for slow-braised bone marrow and traditional Macanese dishes. Or try Nga Tim Cafe on the same Coloane square for a casual Macanese meal outdoors.
Food
19:30
Ferry back to Hong Kong
TurboJET from Taipa Ferry Terminal back to HK. ~60 min. Last ferries run until ~23:00. Arrive Sheung Wan by ~20:30.
Transport
Tips
  • Currency — Macau Pataca (MOP), but HKD accepted everywhere at 1:1
  • Ferry — book TurboJET in advance for weekends; ~HK$175 one way
  • Getting around — Macau peninsula is walkable; bus/taxi to Taipa & Coloane
  • Free shuttles — casino shuttle buses run from ferry terminal to Cotai Strip
  • UNESCO — 30+ sites in the Historic Centre are World Heritage listed
